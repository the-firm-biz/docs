## Platform Fee

- AutoBoy charges an **8% platform fee** for every successful buy.
- The platform fee is taken _in the project token you bought._
- The platform fee is taken _after_ the swap, so it isn't part of the [effective market cap](/mcap-calculation) AutoBoy evaluates your order against.

## Priority Fee

- **Users may choose to pay a priority fee** to have their order prioritised and executed before others. This optional fee is set when creating or updating your order.
- Learn more about Order Execution & Prioritisation here [link to order execution]
- This fee is only taken when AutoBoy successfully fills your order in the correct position, according to its Order Execution priority algo. 

- The priority fee is taken _after_ the swap, so it isn't part of the [effective market cap](/mcap-calculation) AutoBoy evaluates your order against.

## Gas Fee

- AutoBoy **sponsors all gas fees** on Base & Robinhood.

## Sniper Fees

- Executed swaps incur Sniper fees & LP fees as defined by the pool.
- Sniper & LP Fees are factored into the current token price when AutoBoy evaluates whether a user's order parameters are met. See [Market cap calculation](/mcap-calculation)
