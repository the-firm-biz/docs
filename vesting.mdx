---
title: "Locking tokens (vesting)"
---

When creating an order through AutoBoy, users may opt to lock their tokens to increase the amount they can spend.

After purchase, these tokens are automatically locked for the user-selected time period using [Sablier](https://sablier.com/vesting).

Currently, locking/vesting is a simple '[time lock](https://docs.sablier.com/concepts/lockup/stream-shapes#timelock)'. Once the lock time has passed, a user may withdraw all of their tokens as usual.
